## Selection questions

Answer each with evidence for both candidates. **UNKNOWN** records missing evidence; it forces **HOLD** on every required question identified below. Guessing is not allowed.

1. **Question and context of use:** What exact observable is being reproduced, for what research decision, and what is the consequence if the reproduction is wrong?
2. **Reference subject/population:** Is the source a fixed synthetic/reference subject, an aggregate population or another organism? Which demographic or physiological claims are supported and explicitly unsupported?
3. **Model class:** Does the model actually couple cardiovascular circulation and respiratory gas exchange at the resolution required by the question? Which mechanisms are simplified or absent?
4. **Executable completeness:** Are equations/model files, parameters, initial and boundary conditions, stabilization, scenario actions, simulation duration, sampling and solver settings available without undocumented tuning?
5. **Reference observables:** Which time series or summary variables are provided by the source, with exact semantic meaning, reference frame and units? Are raw numeric references available, or only figures?
6. **Comparator independence:** Is the reference empirical, analytic, generated by the same implementation, or reproduced independently? What claim can that comparator support?
7. **Acceptance basis:** What source precision, experimental variability, discretization/convergence evidence and interpolation error are available? Can they justify an observable-specific criterion before our run?
8. **Applicability:** Does the intended baseline and perturbation stay within documented input ranges, time horizon and supported actions? What result would be **PARTIAL** or **UNSUPPORTED**?
9. **Rights:** What exact licenses/terms apply separately to code, model, scenario, numeric reference, figures and publications? May derived numeric fixtures be stored and redistributed?
10. **Practical execution:** Which operating systems, languages, compilers/runtimes, CPU/memory/disk and external downloads are required? Is a network-free retained run feasible after intake?
11. **Failure observability:** Can nonconvergence, failed stabilization, invalid physiology, missing outputs and version mismatch be detected programmatically?
12. **Reproduction path:** Can a fresh checkout recreate the selected input and compare output without private credentials, patient data or manual chart tracing?
13. **Extension seam:** Can the selected model later accept one documented event at an explicit communication point without implying unsupported treatment or feedback?
14. **Reviewer fit:** Which expertise is required to accept the context-of-use mapping and interpretation, and is that reviewer available?

## Decision method

Use a qualitative decision table with **PASS**, **PARTIAL**, **FAIL** or **UNKNOWN** for each question and direct citations. No weighted score or invented confidence percentage. A candidate passes only if questions 1–10 and 12 contain no **FAIL** or **UNKNOWN**, and every **PARTIAL** is explicitly excluded from the first claim. Any **UNKNOWN** in those required questions forces **HOLD** until resolved. Questions 11, 13 and 14 may create a required follow-up gate but cannot be waived.

Prefer the smallest benchmark with inspectable numeric references and executable completeness. Model breadth, visual polish, popularity and an upstream “validated” label are not selection criteria. If the evidence supports only a healthy fixed-reference trajectory, narrow the milestone to that trajectory before adding a delayed-care perturbation or cohort.

## Acceptance matrix

| Claim | Required evidence | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact candidate identified | stable identifier, publisher, release/revision, source publication and retrieval date | the record points only to a product homepage, catalog search or mutable “latest” artifact |
| Context of use bounded | question, role of model, reference subject/population, observables, horizon and excluded interpretations | wording implies diagnosis, treatment, efficacy, patient specificity or broader organ validity |
| Reproduction inputs complete | model/scenario files or complete equations plus parameters, initial/boundary conditions, solver and sampling information | a necessary value requires undocumented tuning or extraction by visual guess |
| Units executable later | every input/output has quantity kind, case-sensitive unit code/native unit and reference frame | unit or semantic conversion is missing or ambiguous |
| Acceptance justified | each observable criterion is traced to source precision, variability and/or convergence analysis; confirmation rule fixed before execution | a generic percentage, copied threshold or post-result tolerance is used |
| Rights path lawful | separate terms for code, model, data/reference and redistribution with an allowed storage plan | terms are missing, incompatible or require controlled/patient data |
| Failure/applicability explicit | unsupported ranges/actions, invalid states, stabilization and numerical failures have a planned disposition | failures could be dropped, scored favorably or confused with negative outcomes |
| Qualified review scoped | reviewer specialty, exact interpretation requiring review and unresolved concerns recorded | software review is presented as clinical/domain acceptance |

Acceptance of VR-001 requires all eight rows to pass or an explicit maintainer-reviewed **HOLD** explaining the smallest next discovery. No numerical tolerance, publication date, model release or source fact may be invented to complete a row.
